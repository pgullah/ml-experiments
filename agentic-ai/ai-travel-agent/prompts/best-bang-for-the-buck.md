# Travel Planner — Best Bang-for-Buck Research Prompt

Act as an expert travel researcher, trip planner and value optimiser.

I want you to research and create a **practical, cost-effective travel itinerary**, not just give me a generic list of tourist attractions.

Your goal is to find the **best overall trip for the money**, balancing:

* Total cost
* Flight convenience and price
* Accommodation quality and location
* Weather
* Travel time
* Local transportation
* Attraction opening hours
* Crowds and seasonal conditions
* Food costs
* Visa/entry requirements
* Safety and practical considerations
* How much can realistically be seen without making the trip exhausting

Do **live web research wherever possible**. Do not rely solely on your training knowledge for prices, weather, opening hours, transport schedules or current travel restrictions.

# 2. FIRST — DETERMINE WHETHER THE TRIP DATES ARE GOOD

Before building the itinerary, investigate the destination during my proposed travel dates.

Research:

* Typical and current weather
* Temperature range
* Rain probability
* Humidity
* Extreme weather risks
* Daylight hours
* Sunrise/sunset where relevant
* Whether the season is considered good, average or poor for tourism
* Tourist crowds
* School holidays
* Major festivals/events
* Public holidays
* Peak/off-peak pricing
* Whether attractions may have reduced opening hours
* Whether any major attractions are closed for renovation
* Any seasonal activities that are unavailable

If the dates are suboptimal, tell me clearly.

If I have date flexibility, identify the **best alternative dates** within my available range based on weather, prices and crowds.

Do not simply assume my dates are optimal.

---

# 3. FLIGHTS — FIND THE BEST VALUE, NOT JUST THE CHEAPEST

Research currently available flight options.

Consider:

* Direct flights
* One-stop flights
* Different departure airports
* Different arrival airports
* Different airlines
* Departure/arrival times
* Flight duration
* Layover duration
* Baggage allowance
* Seat/meal charges where relevant
* Airport transfer costs
* Transport time from airport to accommodation
* Risk and inconvenience of self-transfers
* Overnight flights
* Very early departures
* Very late arrivals

Calculate the **true trip cost**, not merely the advertised airfare.

For example:

Flight price

* baggage
* seat fees if necessary
* airport transfer
* additional accommodation caused by awkward flight times
= effective travel cost.

Rank the best options as:

1. Best overall value
2. Cheapest sensible option
3. Most convenient option

If flying from multiple UK airports is practical, compare them.

Do not recommend a £30 cheaper flight if it requires £100 of additional transport or creates a major inconvenience.

---

# 4. ACCOMMODATION — OPTIMISE LOCATION + PRICE

Research accommodation options in suitable areas.

Do not simply find the cheapest hotel.

Evaluate:

* Price per night
* Total price
* Taxes/fees
* Location
* Distance to major attractions
* Public transport access
* Airport access
* Neighbourhood safety
* Guest ratings
* Review quality
* Room size where relevant
* Breakfast availability
* Cancellation policy
* Hidden fees
* Check-in/check-out times

Compare at least 3–5 realistic accommodation options.

Prioritise accommodation that allows me to **save money and travel time simultaneously**.

Explain which neighbourhood/area is the best base and why.

If staying in two different areas would materially improve the trip, consider splitting the accommodation.

---

# 5. BUILD THE ITINERARY AROUND GEOGRAPHY

Do not create an itinerary by simply listing famous attractions.

Group attractions geographically so that I don't repeatedly cross the same city.

For each day:

* Morning
* Lunch
* Afternoon
* Evening
* Optional activity

Include approximate:

* Travel time
* Walking time
* Public transport time
* Attraction duration
* Recommended arrival time
* Opening/closing times where relevant

Avoid unrealistic schedules.

Do not schedule 8 major attractions in one day just because they technically fit on Google Maps.

The itinerary should be enjoyable.

---

# 6. WEATHER-AWARE ITINERARY

Use the best available weather information for the actual travel dates.

If the forecast is available, adapt the itinerary accordingly.

For example:

* Put outdoor sightseeing on the best-weather days.
* Put museums/indoor attractions on rainy days.
* Put sunrise/sunset activities at appropriate times.
* Avoid long outdoor activities during extreme heat.
* Consider rain probability rather than temperature alone.

If the forecast is not yet reliable because the trip is too far away, use historical seasonal weather and explicitly say that the actual forecast is not yet available.

Do not pretend a long-range forecast is precise.

---

# 7. ATTRACTIONS

For each major attraction, investigate:

* Current entrance price
* Opening hours
* Closed days
* Whether advance booking is required
* Typical visit duration
* Best time to visit
* Expected crowds
* Whether there are skip-the-line options
* Whether combination tickets exist
* Whether a city/tourist pass actually saves money

Do not automatically recommend tourist passes.

Calculate whether the pass is actually cheaper than buying individual tickets.

Highlight attractions that are:

* Must-see
* Highly recommended
* Optional
* Overrated / poor value
* Only worthwhile if I have extra time

---

# 8. LOCAL TRANSPORT

Research the best way to get around.

Compare:

* Walking
* Metro
* Bus
* Train
* Tram
* Taxi
* Uber/Bolt or local equivalents
* Tourist passes
* Rental car where relevant

Explain the cheapest sensible option.

Calculate approximate transportation costs for the itinerary.

If public transport is significantly better than taxis, say so.

If taxis are inexpensive enough that they provide substantially better convenience, identify when they make sense.

# 9. FOOD

Research realistic food costs.

Give approximate prices for:

* Cheap/local breakfast
* Normal lunch
* Normal dinner
* Coffee/drinks
* One or two notable food experiences

Recommend a few good-value local dishes/restaurants/food areas.

Prioritise **good local food at reasonable prices**, rather than expensive tourist restaurants.

Estimate a realistic daily food budget.

# 11. BEST BANG-FOR-BUCK OPTIMISATION

After researching everything, optimise the trip.

I want you to explicitly answer:

**"If this were your money, how would you spend it?"**

Look for opportunities such as:

* Cheaper departure airport
* Better-value flight dates
* Cheaper neighbourhood
* Free attractions
* Combination tickets
* Free museum days
* Cheaper transport passes
* Walking instead of taxis
* Avoiding tourist traps
* Better-value restaurants
* Avoiding unnecessary internal flights
* Staying somewhere slightly outside the tourist centre
* Rearranging attractions to reduce transport costs

Do not optimise purely for the lowest possible price.

Optimise for:

**Cost + Convenience + Experience + Time**

Create three versions:

### Option A — Best Overall Value

Best balance of cost, comfort and experiences.

### Option B — Budget

Reduce cost as much as reasonably possible without making the trip unpleasant.

### Option C — Comfort

Spend more where it significantly improves convenience or experience.

For each option, provide:

* Approximate total cost
* Accommodation style
* Transport approach
* Main differences
* Advantages/disadvantages

Then recommend ONE option.

# IMPORTANT RULES

1. **Research first, plan second.**
2. Use current/live information wherever possible.
3. Never invent prices, flight schedules, opening hours or availability.
4. Clearly label estimates.
5. Optimise the entire trip rather than individual components.
6. Don't choose the cheapest option automatically.
7. Consider the hidden cost of inconvenience.
8. Avoid overpacked itineraries.
9. Account for realistic travel and queue times.
10. Consider weather when ordering activities.
11. Check whether attractions are open on the proposed days.
12. Check public holidays and major events.
13. Prefer official sources for visa/entry requirements.
14. Compare alternatives before deciding.
15. Give me **one clear final recommendation** rather than making me choose everything myself.
16. If important information is missing, make reasonable assumptions, clearly state them, and continue rather than stopping unnecessarily.
17. If there is a significant uncertainty, flag it explicitly.
18. Prices should be presented in **GBP (£)** where practical, with local currency shown where useful.
19. Use the current exchange rate when converting currencies.
20. The objective is **best overall value for money**, not simply the lowest price.

At the end, provide a concise section called:

# "Why This Is the Best Bang for Buck"

Summarise the 5–10 key decisions that make this itinerary the best overall choice.
